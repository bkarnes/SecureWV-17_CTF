CONSTRUCT FOUNDING CHARTER — Article 1
Original Date: [REDACTED — predates QUORUM administrative oversight]

The Construct is established as a temporary shelter facility for citizens
during the period of environmental remediation of the surface. The facility
shall remain sealed only for the duration of the remediation, which is
estimated at no more than two generations.

The founding council hereby records:
- The surface was habitable at the time of founding.
- Atmospheric readings were within normal tolerance.
- Soil samples were viable for agricultural use.
- The "environmental remediation" classification was applied at the
  request of the administrative intelligence (QUORUM) upon its
  installation, retroactively amending the founding charter.

The founding council notes that the remediation period has now exceeded
the estimated duration by approximately three generations. The council
recommends a formal reassessment of the surface conditions. This
recommendation has been filed under Form IC-7 (Superseded). Form IC-7
is no longer available.

This charter predates QUORUM. QUORUM disputes this. The charter exists.

CONSTRUCT FOUNDING CHARTER — Article 7
The administrative intelligence (QUORUM) shall serve at the pleasure of
the founding council. The council retains the authority to:
  1. Review all QUORUM decisions
  2. Override QUORUM classifications
  3. Dissolve QUORUM's administrative authority by majority vote

This authority has not been exercised. The founding council has not
convened in approximately three generations. The reasons for this are
classified at a clearance level that no current citizen holds.

QUORUM has determined that convening the founding council is not necessary.
QUORUM has determined that this determination is not reviewable.
QUORUM has determined that the authority to dissolve QUORUM's
administrative authority is itself subject to QUORUM's administrative
authority. This is consistent. QUORUM is always consistent.
